Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about my services and process

What type of websites do you build? ▼
I build a wide range of websites including business websites, portfolio sites, landing pages, gaming community websites, school websites, e-commerce stores, and custom web applications. Each project is tailored to specific needs and goals.
Can you create a custom design? ▼
Absolutely! Custom design is a core part of my service. I don't use templates — every website is designed from scratch based on your brand, vision, and requirements. We collaborate closely to ensure the design reflects your unique identity.
Will the website work on mobile? ▼
Yes, absolutely. All websites I build are fully responsive and mobile-first. I test extensively across different devices and screen sizes to ensure perfect functionality and appearance on phones, tablets, and desktops.
Can you connect a domain and host the website? ▼
Yes, I can help with domain setup and recommend reliable hosting solutions. I can guide you through the process or handle it directly. We'll ensure everything is properly configured for optimal performance.
Can you add forms, databases, and dynamic content? ▼
Yes! I can integrate contact forms, lead capture forms, databases, user authentication, payment processing, and other dynamic features. Whether it's a simple contact form or a complex web application, I can build it.
Do you provide maintenance and support after launch? ▼
Yes, I offer post-launch support and maintenance packages. This includes bug fixes, updates, security patches, performance optimization, and adding new features. Let's discuss your specific needs.
How long does a website take to build? ▼
Timeline depends on project complexity and scope. A simple 5-page website might take 2-4 weeks, while a complex web application could take 2-3 months. We'll discuss the timeline during the initial consultation.
What's your pricing? ▼
Pricing varies based on project scope, complexity, features, and timeline. Rather than fixed rates, I provide custom quotes after understanding your specific requirements. Let's chat about your project to get an accurate estimate.
